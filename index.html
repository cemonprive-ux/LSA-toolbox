Must-see places in town

The 17 sights every visitor to Clermont-Ferrand should know, plus 5 extras. Tap “Show on map” to find a place, or “Directions” to get there from where you are.

City centre 10

On foot from the school

Place de Jaude

The city’s main square, lined with shops and cafés. Look for the bronze statue of Vercingétorix, the Gallic chief, by Bartholdi, the sculptor of the Statue of Liberty.

Show on map Directions

Notre-Dame-de-l’Assomption Cathedral

A Gothic cathedral built from dark Volvic lava stone. Work began in 1248; its two spires were only completed in the 19th century by Viollet-le-Duc.

Show on map Directions

Notre-Dame-du-Port Basilica

A 12th-century Romanesque basilica in pale stone, listed by UNESCO as part of the Routes of Santiago de Compostela. Go down to the crypt.

Show on map Directions

Les Salins flea market

A huge flea market every Sunday morning, 7 am to 1 pm, on the Place Gambetta car park in the Salins district. Free entry; come early for the best finds.

Show on map Directions

Jardin Lecoq

An English-style garden right in the centre, with a pond, a rose garden and tall old trees. The ideal break between two visits.

Show on map Directions

FRAC Auvergne

The regional contemporary art collection, with a strong focus on painting. Exhibitions change through the year, a few steps from the cathedral.

Show on map Directions

Taste the local food

Try truffade, aligot and Auvergne cheeses such as Saint-Nectaire. The Halle gourmande Saint-Pierre, the covered market in the city centre, is a good place to start.

Show on map Directions

Tourist office, Place de la Victoire Extra

Free maps, guided tours and up-to-date opening times, right beside the cathedral.

Show on map Directions

Henri-Lecoq Natural History Museum Extra

Next to the Jardin Lecoq: rocks and volcanoes, fossils, plants and the wildlife of Auvergne.

Show on map Directions

Bargoin Museum Extra

Archaeology from Gallo-Roman Clermont, alongside a collection of textile art from around the world.

Montferrand 4

Tram A from the centre

The old historic quarter of Clermont-Ferrand. Montferrand began as a separate town, founded around 1120 by the Counts of Auvergne as a “new town” to rival Clermont, the bishops’ city next door. It received one of the region’s earliest charters of freedoms in the late 12th century.

King Louis XIII ordered the two towns to unite in 1630 (Edict of Troyes), and Louis XV made the union effective in 1731, which gave the city its double name. Its streets still hold Gothic and Renaissance mansions, and it was one of the first protected historic districts in France.

Medieval Montferrand

Stroll through the medieval street plan, past Gothic and Renaissance mansions with carved façades. Do not miss the Maison de l’Éléphant.

Show on map Directions

Roger-Quilliot Art Museum (MARQ)

The city’s fine-arts museum, set in a former convent in Montferrand: painting and sculpture from the Middle Ages to the 20th century, including some very large canvases.

Show on map Directions

L’Aventure Michelin

An interactive museum about Michelin, founded in Clermont in 1889: tyres, travel maps, the famous guides and the Michelin Man.

Show on map Directions

ASM Experience Extra

The museum of ASM Clermont Auvergne, the city’s rugby club, next to the Marcel-Michelin stadium.

Around the city 4

Bus, car or a good walk

Montjuzet Park

Clermont’s largest park, on a hill north of the centre: terraced gardens, a small Mediterranean garden and wide views over the city and the volcanoes.

Show on map Directions

Pierre Carrée viewpoint

The spot where locals come to see the whole of Clermont spread out below, with the cathedral at its heart. Beautiful at sunset and at night.

Show on map Directions

Wellness in Royat

Royat, the spa town next door, has been known for its springs since Roman times. Its Royatonic centre offers thermal pools, saunas and hammams.

Show on map Directions

SENSAS, an unusual experience

A team game built around your senses: touch, hearing and smell are put to the test in a series of challenges. Fun with friends; book ahead.

Show on map Directions

Volcanoes & countryside 4

Car, or seasonal shuttle

Puy de Dôme summit

At 1,465 m, the highest volcano of the Chaîne des Puys, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Take the Panoramique des Dômes rack train (about 15 minutes) or hike up; at the top, views over the volcanoes and a Roman temple of Mercury.

Show on map Directions

Puy de Pariou

A textbook volcano with an almost perfectly round crater. A loop of about 6 km starts from the Goules car park in Orcines. Stay on the marked paths: the crater floor is off-limits.

Show on map Directions

Vulcania

A science park about volcanoes and planet Earth, about 15 km from Clermont: immersive films, attractions and a planetarium. Open from late March to early November.

Show on map Directions

Gergovie plateau Extra

Where Vercingétorix defeated Julius Caesar in 52 BC. A museum tells the story; the plateau offers walks and wide views. About 15 minutes south by car.
