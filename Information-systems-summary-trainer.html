Millions could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to AI

Incorporating artificial intelligence (AI) into workflows could mean we work less for the same pay, according to the think tank Autonomy.

Nearly a third of the UK's workforce could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to the rollout of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) technology, a new report claims.

One of the biggest concerns around the increasing power and popularity of AI tech over the past couple of years is whether it will take jobs away from humans, as it is proven to be capable of taking on more of the tasks that humans currently perform.

A recent survey from LinkedIn found nearly half of European workers are expecting AI to make a major impact on their working lives within the next year.

Generative text AI tools like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Bard can write whole articles or business plans in seconds, producing a close approximation of what a human can do when fed the right prompts and information. Image generation tools meanwhile can conjure up photorealistic pictures nearly instantaneously, raising questions about job security for human artists and illustrators.

But the new report from Autonomy, an independent think tank that looks at the future of work and economic planning, sets out a more promising vision of AI's impact on our working lives.

In its GPT-4 report, the authors suggest 8.8 million workers in the UK — 28 per cent of the workforce — could be working 32-hour, four-day weeks thanks to AI. A further 27.9 million meanwhile — representing 88 per cent of the country's workforce — could have working hours reduced by at least 10 per cent too.

Autonomy made its calculations using the 1.5 per cent annual productivity increase from AI that was estimated by Goldman Sachs in a recent study on the topic of AI and GDP, as well as data from the US Department of Labor's Occupational Information Network (O*NET).

The Autonomy report outlines two forecasts for leveraging AI to boost productivity while delivering better conditions for workers, specifically looking at how the ideas could be rolled out in local authorities in the UK. The two scenarios are a 20 per cent reduction in working hours while keeping the same pay for workers, or one in which worker productivity increases by 10 per cent, enabling a 10 per cent reduction in working hours, while keeping the same pay.

"Our research offers a fresh perspective in debates around how AI can be utilised for good," said Will Stronge, director of research at Autonomy. "A shorter working week is the most tangible way of ensuring that AI delivers benefits to workers as well as companies. If AI is to be implemented fairly across the economy, it should usher in a new era of four-day working weeks for all".

Various four-day week trials are either underway or have been recently completed, with the majority of results pointing towards either maintained or improved levels of productivity, with substantial benefits for worker well-being.

Source: Euronews.com, "Millions could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to AI" — Luke Hurst, 11 November 2023.

Step 1 / 7

Understand

Before you can summarise a text, you need to separate two things that students often confuse: what the text is about, and what the author thinks about it.

Topic

The subject the text discusses. Neutral — it doesn't tell you the author's opinion.

Author's position

What the author actually argues or concludes about that topic.

TOPIC ≠ AUTHOR'S POSITION — a good summary always states both, separately.

Step 4 / 7

Reformulate

Four reusable techniques to put ideas into your own words — the skill you'll need for every future summary, not just this one.

4.1 — Synonym

Replace tangible with a suitable synonym and rewrite the sentence.
"A shorter working week is the most tangible way of ensuring that AI delivers benefits to workers."
Model — A shorter working week is the most concrete way of ensuring that AI delivers benefits to workers.

4.2 — Change the structure

Rewrite this sentence in the passive voice.
"Autonomy published a report on AI and the four-day week."
Model — A report on AI and the four-day week was published by Autonomy.

4.3 — Generalise

These three sources share something in common. Name the category in a few words.
Goldman Sachs · the US Department of Labor · LinkedIn
Model — external data sources

4.4 — Combine

Merge these three short sentences into one.
"AI could reduce working hours. AI could keep pay the same. AI could improve worker wellbeing."
Model — AI could reduce working hours and improve worker wellbeing while keeping pay the same.

Model answer

The article examines a report by the think tank Autonomy suggesting that AI could let millions of UK workers move to a four-day week. The author explains that although AI's growing power raises widespread fears of job losses, Autonomy offers a more hopeful view. It also points out that a significant share of the UK workforce could work fewer hours for the same pay by 2033, according to the report. Another point is that these estimates rely on productivity data from external sources such as Goldman Sachs and the US Department of Labor. The report's director of research argues that a shorter week is the clearest way to share AI's benefits fairly between workers and companies. Finally, the article concludes that early four-day week trials already show steady or improved productivity, along with real gains in employee wellbeing.

Summary Method

  1. Understand

    What is the text about? What is the author's position?

  2. Select

    Keep main ideas. Remove unnecessary evidence.

  3. Condense

    Combine several details into one broader idea.

  4. Reformulate

    Use your own words and structures.

  5. Organise

    Build a logical plan.

  6. Write

    Be concise, accurate and neutral.

  7. Check

    Content, selection, reformulation, organisation, language and word count.
