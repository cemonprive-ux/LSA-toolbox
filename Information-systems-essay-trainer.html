Discussion question
How will the emergence of new technologies reshape the professional world of tomorrow?
Source Document

Millions could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to AI

Incorporating artificial intelligence (AI) into workflows could mean we work less for the same pay, according to the think tank Autonomy.

Nearly a third of the UK's workforce could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to the rollout of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) technology, a new report claims.

One of the biggest concerns around the increasing power and popularity of AI tech over the past couple of years is whether it will take jobs away from humans, as it is proven to be capable of taking on more of the tasks that humans currently perform.

A recent survey from LinkedIn found nearly half of European workers are expecting AI to make a major impact on their working lives within the next year.

Generative text AI tools like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Bard can write whole articles or business plans in seconds, producing a close approximation of what a human can do when fed the right prompts and information. Image generation tools meanwhile can conjure up photorealistic pictures nearly instantaneously, raising questions about job security for human artists and illustrators.

But the new report from Autonomy, an independent think tank that looks at the future of work and economic planning, sets out a more promising vision of AI's impact on our working lives.

In its GPT-4 report, the authors suggest 8.8 million workers in the UK — 28 per cent of the workforce — could be working 32-hour, four-day weeks thanks to AI. A further 27.9 million meanwhile — representing 88 per cent of the country's workforce — could have working hours reduced by at least 10 per cent too.

Autonomy made its calculations using the 1.5 per cent annual productivity increase from AI that was estimated by Goldman Sachs in a recent study on the topic of AI and GDP, as well as data from the US Department of Labor's Occupational Information Network (O*NET).

The Autonomy report outlines two forecasts for leveraging AI to boost productivity while delivering better conditions for workers, specifically looking at how the ideas could be rolled out in local authorities in the UK. The two scenarios are a 20 per cent reduction in working hours while keeping the same pay for workers, or one in which worker productivity increases by 10 per cent, enabling a 10 per cent reduction in working hours, while keeping the same pay.

"Our research offers a fresh perspective in debates around how AI can be utilised for good," said Will Stronge, director of research at Autonomy. "A shorter working week is the most tangible way of ensuring that AI delivers benefits to workers as well as companies. If AI is to be implemented fairly across the economy, it should usher in a new era of four-day working weeks for all".

Various four-day week trials are either underway or have been recently completed, with the majority of results pointing towards either maintained or improved levels of productivity, with substantial benefits for worker well-being.

Source: Euronews.com, "Millions could be working a four-day week by 2033 thanks to AI" — Luke Hurst, 11 November 2023.

05 Build a Strong Argument

Un argument oral efficace suit quatre temps : IDEA → EXPLAIN → EXAMPLE → LINK.

Why this matters
Idea only — too weakNew technology makes work more efficient.
+ Explain — betterNew technology makes work more efficient because AI can now handle repetitive tasks that used to take employees hours to complete.
+ Example — stronger…For example, Autonomy's report suggests AI-driven productivity gains could let up to 28% of UK workers move to a four-day week without losing pay.
Part 1 — Transformation of work Build one argument

Modèle — Part 1

Idea — Automation and AI could streamline routine tasks and let employees work fewer hours for the same pay.

Explain — Because AI-powered tools can now handle repetitive or data-heavy processes far faster than humans, freeing up time.

Example — For example, Autonomy's report suggests up to 28 per cent of UK workers could move to a 32-hour, four-day week by 2033 thanks to these productivity gains, and completed trials already show maintained or improved output.

Link — This shows that new technologies can genuinely reorganise how — and how much — people work, not just what tasks they do.

Part 2 — New demands and risks Build one argument

Modèle — Part 2

Idea — New technologies require professionals to constantly acquire new skills to remain employable.

Explain — Because generative tools can now perform tasks that used to require specific human expertise, so yesterday's skill set can quickly become insufficient.

Example — For example, the text notes that generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and image generators already raise real job-security concerns for writers and illustrators.

Link — This shows that, without continuous upskilling and growing expertise in areas like cybersecurity, professionals risk being left behind by this transformation.

07 Prepare Your Introduction

À l'oral, l'introduction reste courte : position → problème principal → annonce du plan, en 3 phrases directes.

Which sentence is the best opening line?
Build your 3-line oral introductionUne phrase par ligne. Restez court — c'est ce que vous direz, pas ce que vous lirez.

Modèle — introduction orale

New technologies are likely to transform the professional world significantly, but this transformation will not affect every worker in the same way.

They offer real gains in efficiency and flexibility, but they also demand new skills and raise new risks.

I will first consider how work is being reorganised before examining what this demands from professionals.

08 Nuance Your Answer

« How will X reshape Y » n'accepte jamais une réponse tout-ou-rien. Utilisez may, can, could, to some extent, depending on, unless…

Too binaryArtificial intelligence will destroy jobs.
NuancedArtificial intelligence may automate certain tasks, but whether this destroys jobs overall will depend on how far companies invest in reskilling and how quickly new roles emerge.
Rewrite this claim with more nuance:« Automation makes jobs disappear. » Utilisez au moins un mot de nuance.

Modèle — nuance

Automation can eliminate certain repetitive tasks, but it does not necessarily destroy jobs overall — much depends on whether displaced workers are given the chance to reskill and move into new roles.

Introduction

New technologies are likely to transform the professional world significantly, but this transformation will not affect every worker in the same way.   • A further 88% could see their hours cut by at least 10% while keeping the same pay.Source : texte, §7

Part 3. Conditions for success
  • Autonomy's director of research argues fairness is not automatic: "if AI is to be implemented fairly across the economy, it should usher in a new era of four-day working weeks for all."Source : texte
  • Businesses will increasingly need to integrate sustainability practices into their operations.Source : connaissances
  • Adaptability, continuous learning and a proactive approach to new technology will be key to thriving.Source : connaissances
  • Autonomy's calculations rely on a modest 1.5% annual productivity gain (per Goldman Sachs), suggesting gradual rather than sudden change.Source : texte

Conclusion

Overall, new technologies are likely to reshape the professional world in profound ways — reorganising tasks, hours and work arrangements while demanding new technical and ethical skills from professionals. But this transformation is not determined by technology alone: its outcome will depend on how fairly companies and governments manage reskilling, cybersecurity and sustainability. As Autonomy's director of research puts it, AI should "usher in a new era of four-day working weeks for all" — a goal that requires deliberate choices, not just new tools.
